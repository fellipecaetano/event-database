Fact states

Property absent No projected fact under that typed field.
state: "unknown" A Source explicitly stated that the value is unsettled.
state: "known" Read value, confidence, and evidence.
Typed property absent but rawFact() present A schema-valid Override produced the wrong runtime shape; preserve it, do not invent a replacement.

Rules

  • Always pass an explicit at clock.
  • Open a fresh snapshot after a write.
  • Never serialize catalogue entities directly to public output.
  • Use typed camelCase properties; rawFact() uses persisted snake_case names.
  • Catch InvalidCatalogueError when a caller needs custom diagnostics.
