# The catalogue is probabilistic, not deterministic

Event scheduling information in Brazil is chaotic, and we gather it from arbitrary,
uneven Sources that contradict each other and go quiet without warning. Rather than
forcing every field into a single settled truth, each derived fact — including an Event's
Status — is accompanied by a Confidence grounded in the Observation log, and anything
rendering the catalogue is free to express that uncertainty instead of hiding it.

The Observation log is what makes this possible: because we keep everything every Source
ever said, confidence can be computed from real agreement, disagreement and silence rather
than asserted. This is also the differentiator. Competing listings render guesses as facts;
we can say how sure we are.

## Consequences

An Absence — a Listing we knew about no longer being there — is recorded as evidence and
never treated as proof of cancellation. A broken scraper therefore lowers confidence
rather than silently wiping a night of São Paulo out of the catalogue.

Postponement is an edit to an existing Event, not a new one, so its identity and history
survive the change.

Every projected field needs somewhere to carry its Confidence, and consumers must decide
how to present hedged information.

Confidence is expressed as a few ordered tiers derived from explicit rules, not as a number.
The evidence that moves it — corroboration between independent Documents, Source trust for
that particular field, Validation, whether a value is published or an Estimate, and how
recently it was asserted — is categorical rather than probabilistic, and calibrating a number
would need ground truth about thousands of past events that we do not have and may never
have. A score would present a precision the evidence cannot support, which is the failure
this catalogue exists to avoid.

Our measure of quality is therefore calibration per tier — of everything we called
Corroborated, how much proved right — which is answerable from a few hundred events, and
belongs to the version of the rules that produced it.
